## Hardcoded/configuration findings

- `SCORE_WEIGHTS` lives in `analysis.py` and should move to a documented config module.
- Valuation assumptions are hardcoded in `_valuation`: 10% discount rate, 2.5% terminal growth, five projection years, growth clamps, fair-PE bounds, and 45/35/20 blend weights.
- Scoring thresholds for trend, valuation ratios, risk, liquidity, dividend, and verdict labels are hardcoded in helper functions.
- Missing values often become neutral scores around 50. This is safer than fake company data, but it should be represented as unavailable/low-confidence metadata instead of looking like a true neutral measurement.
- The old prediction, news, and financial-statement models are preserved in their own section and should remain excluded from the Phase 1 score until deliberately redesigned.
- DCF now returns a bridge and uses financial-statement diluted shares when available; if missing, it warns when falling back to profile sharesOutstanding.

## Price bug conclusion

Before the fix, displayed `current_price` and `/predict.current_price` came from adjusted daily Yahoo history ending at yesterday's date. That can be materially different from Google Finance, Yahoo quote pages, or after-hours prices on July 21, 2026. The backend now attempts free Yahoo/yfinance quote fields and intraday/pre-post history first, falls back to adjusted daily history, returns quote source/as-of/session metadata, and clearly says the free source is not guaranteed real-time.
## Phase 1 Consistency Fix Addendum

Implemented after the initial audit:

- DCF bridge now uses the same balance-sheet snapshot as the balance-sheet section when available. The bridge returns enterprise value, cash and equivalents, eligible marketable securities, total liquid assets, short-term interest-bearing debt, long-term interest-bearing debt, debt included in bridge, other adjustments, equity value, share count, and per-share value.
- For GOOGL, the runtime check used SEC companyfacts for period end 2026-03-31 and returned cash and equivalents of 38.063B, marketable securities of 88.777B, total liquid assets of 126.840B, and interest-bearing debt of 79.499B. These values align with Alphabet's March 31, 2026 Form 10-Q balance-sheet and financial-instruments disclosures.
- Balance sheet now exposes one-period primary values: period_end, cash_and_equivalents, marketable_securities, total_liquid_assets, current_assets, current_liabilities, current_ratio, short_term_debt, long_term_debt, total_interest_bearing_debt, lease_liabilities, strict_cash_net_debt, liquidity_adjusted_net_cash, debt ratios, source, source fields, warnings, and lease inclusion semantics.
- Dividend yield now uses a decimal internal convention. Example regression: 0.88 annual dividend / 348.85 price = 0.0025226 internally and 0.2523% in the UI. Frontend no longer turns that into 25%.
- Earnings-date selection now sorts provider dates, rejects same-day placeholders as recent earnings, requires recent dates to be in the past and next dates to be future, and prevents identical recent/next dates. The July 2026 Alphabet regression returns next earnings as 2026-07-22 and recent as unavailable when only a current-day placeholder exists.
- Historical price metadata now separates quote_as_of, history_last_trading_date, history_cache_as_of, and trend_calculation_as_of. Trend/chart/returns/volume continue to use the same daily-history dataset.
- Risk now exposes `risk_level` where 100 is high risk and `risk_safety_score` where 100 is safer. Only the safety score contributes positively to the overall score.
- Old stock-price ML prediction is no longer built inside `/analysis` and no longer appears in Phase 1 key points. Old `/predict`, `/api/news`, and `/api/financials` outputs remain preserved only in Previous Predictions / Experimental Signals.
- News headline sentiment is no longer included in the Phase 1 score weights. Headlines remain informational in the main workflow; sentiment counts remain with the preserved old news output.

Remaining follow-up items:

- Move scoring and valuation constants into a documented config module.
- Add richer period metadata to every financial statement metric, not only the balance-sheet and price/history sections.
- Replace the simple one-year FCF growth input with a documented normalized multi-year/TTM growth rule.
- Add a broader SEC filing parser fallback for concepts not available through companyfacts.
